2Gaps to fix before a public release

These are not guesses; each one is visible in the files.
  • Music and all UI images have no recorded source. The credits screen calls the music "original loops, made for this game" but nobody has a record of who made it or with what tool. The files carry a C2PA tag saying they passed through Claude's file handling, which only shows how they were copied, not who made them. The owner should confirm where Mira, the world pictures, the backdrop, stars, icons and both music loops came from.
  • wordfreq (CC BY-SA 4.0) is not credited on the credits screen. That license requires credit. Add "Word list: wordfreq by Robyn Speer (CC BY-SA 4.0)".
  • The credits screen says "Full list in CREDITS.md", but that file was lost and is not in the app. Point it to this page or ship a new CREDITS file.
  • The font license texts are not shipped. The SIL OFL and Apache 2.0 both ask for the license text to travel with the font files. Add OFL.txt and the Apache license next to fonts/.
  • Stories have no author credit. Several are published children's stories (for example "The Day the Crayons Quit" and "Owen and Mzee"). The research team should confirm they may be used and how to credit them.
  • Teacher Mira has no credit line on the credits screen, and no repo LICENSE says who owns the game code.

Lines that have no clip

All 80 lines in Mira's main line lists have clips. These fixed lines in the code do not, so they use the phone voice:

  • Time's up! Here is the right order. Read it once, then let's write your summary.
  • Last stage! Write 2 or 3 sentences that tell the story in your own words. Before you check, ask yourself: …
  • Tell me more! Write 2 or 3 sentences: who the story is about, what happened, and how it ended.
  • Some words are repeated too many times. Tell the story in sentences that make sense.
  • Write it in sentences, not a list of words. Use words like wanted, but, so, and then.
  • I see only one sentence. Please write 2 or 3 sentences, with a period at the end of each one.
  • Some details don't match the story. Look at the red lines and fix them.
  • Who is the story about? Name the main character in your summary.
  • Your summary needs the main idea. What is the whole story mostly about?
  • Good effort! Your teacher will read your summary and help you with it.

Lines built from a template (with a name, number or story title) were not checked one by one and may also fall back.
