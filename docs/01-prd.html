Product Requirements Document

SummaRace v3 · version 1.3.6 · Android app com.summarace.app

1Overview

This document says what SummaRace does, feature by feature, and how each feature is expected to behave. It describes the game as it is built today, not a wish list.

SummaRace is a reading game for Grade 4 learners. For each story the learner reads it page by page, races through its story parts in a 3D runner, puts the parts in order, and then sums it up in 2–3 sentences. The tagline on the title screen is "Read it · Race it · Sum it up!". Teacher Mira, a cartoon teacher, guides the learner with spoken lines the whole way.

2Product goal

Help Grade 4 learners practise reading comprehension and summarising with the Somebody – Wanted – But – So – Then (SWBST) method, in a game they want to keep playing, and give the teacher a record of every finished level that they can open in a spreadsheet.

The game has to work on low-cost Android phones in a classroom with no internet and no accounts.

3Users

Learner

A Grade 4 pupil. Types their name and section, plays the stories in order, earns stars and coins.

Teacher

Hands out the phones, reads the records screen, exports the CSV, can erase or bring back records.

Research team (client)

Wrote the 30 stories, questions and SWBST answers. Uses the CSV results for the study.

4Content scope

  • 30 stories in 10 sessions. Each session has one easy, one average and one hard story (levels 1–30).
  • Each story has 5 pages, 5 page questions (one per page), 1 main idea question, and 5 SWBST story parts with right and wrong answers.
  • The 30 levels sit in 5 worlds of 6 levels each: Sunny Meadow, River Bend, Story Town, Palm Beach and Summit Peak.
  • The pages, page questions, SWBST answers and main idea come word for word from the research team. The wrong answer choices, race wording and Teacher Mira's example summaries were written for the game.

5Features

FeatureDescriptionExpected behaviour
Title screenAnimated title with Teacher Mira, a PLAY button and Credits.PLAY opens "Who is playing?". Credits opens the credits popup.
Learner profilesName (required, max 40 characters) and section (optional, max 30).A new name and section makes a new profile on this device (matching ignores capital letters). Returning learners tap their name under "Or continue as". Progress is saved per profile.
World mapA scrolling path of 30 level nodes with a session bar (sessions 1–10) and a "go to my level" button.Levels unlock one by one. Tapping a locked level shows a card that sends the learner to their open level. Stars and best score show on finished levels.
ReadOne page at a time with a world picture, page dots and a Listen button.Mira says a short tip and then reads the page aloud with a recorded voice. "Answer the question" goes to that page's question.
Page questions3 answer choices in a new random order every time.A wrong answer is never revealed. After a wrong answer Mira offers "Read the story" or "Try again". After 2 wrong tries the story opens with the right part highlighted. Only a right first try counts for the score.
Main ideaAfter the last page, 4 choices: which one tells what the whole story is about.Same retry rules as page questions. Scored right or wrong on the first try.
RaceA 3D endless runner: the learner's runner (Max or Mia) escapes a police officer and dog.Each SWBST part becomes a question at the top with answers A, B and C shown in the 3 lanes. The learner swipes into the lane of the right answer and runs through its gate. Right = boost and the patrol falls back. Wrong = the patrol comes closer and the question comes back later. Trains and barriers must be dodged (swipe left/right, up to jump, down to roll). If the patrol catches the runner, the race restarts.
Story orderTap the 5 story parts into the Somebody, Wanted, But, So, Then slots.Right parts turn green and stay. After the first wrong check a 30-second clock starts (it pauses while the story or Settings is open). When time runs out Mira shows the right order and the level goes on. Score = parts right on the first check.
SummaryWrite 2–3 sentences in English, in your own words.An offline checker gives feedback lines (see TRD). It passes when the summary names the main character, has most story parts and the main idea, and has no clear mistakes. After 3 tries Mira shows an example. After 4 tries the learner can choose "Finish, my teacher will check".
ResultsStars (1–3), a title, the score for each stage and coins earned.Total ≥ 85% = 3 stars "AMAZING!", ≥ 65% = 2 stars "GREAT JOB!", otherwise 1 star "GOOD TRY!". Buttons: Map, Replay, Next. The next level unlocks.
Teacher recordsA table of every finished level on this device.Copy CSV, Download CSV (saved to Download/SummaRace/ on Android), Share CSV (Android share sheet). "Erase all records" needs the word ERASE typed in; the last erase can be undone with "Bring back erased records".
SettingsTeacher voice, sound effects and music switches; Change player; Teacher records; Quit this story; Exit game.Switches are saved on the device. Quit is offered during a level, Exit game only inside the Android app.
Teacher MiraA guide docked at the bottom of every screen except the race and records.Speaks recorded lines and shows a speech bubble. The bubble folds away after 7 seconds, or after 2 seconds if it covers a button the learner needs. Tap her to hear the line again.
Android Back buttonHandled by the game, not the system.Closes a popup first. In the race, the first Back pauses. During a level it opens the Quit popup instead of dropping progress. On the map it goes back to the title screen.

7Constraints

  • Fully offline. No server, no login, no analytics, no ads. Everything ships inside the APK.
  • Android 7.0 or newer (minSdk 24), portrait only, phones with about 2 GB of RAM.
  • Records live only on the device they were made on. The teacher must collect the CSV from each phone.
  • The summary checker must run offline, so it uses word lists and rules, not an AI model.

8Out of scope

  • Syncing records between devices or to the cloud.
  • Teacher accounts, passwords or a class dashboard.
  • Languages other than English (the summary must be written in English).
  • iOS builds (the web game would run in a browser, but no iOS app is made).
