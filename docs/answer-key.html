1How to read this key

The game shuffles the answer choices every time, so the letter (A, B, C) changes from play to play. This key gives the answer text, not a letter.

  • Page questions: one per page, 3 choices. The right answer is in bold; the other two choices are listed after it.
  • Main idea: 4 choices, the right one is given.
  • Story parts (SWBST): the right answers are the same for the race (pick the lane) and for Story order (put them Somebody → Then). The 2 wrong race choices are listed after each.
  • Model summary: Teacher Mira's example, shown after the 3rd summary try. Any summary in the learner's own words that tells these parts passes; it does not have to match.

This key is in a public repository, by the owner's choice. Learners who find it could look up the answers.
